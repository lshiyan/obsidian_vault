- In zero-sum games, optimal policies that jointly maximize utility are proven to exist and are called minimax-optimal joint policies, also Nash equilibria. $\pi^* = (\pi_1^* ,  \pi_2^*)$. They are also interchangeable, given two optimal polices $\pi^{*}, \pi^{*'}$, then you can exchange out any policy from the prime policy with the opposite from the base policy and they would be optimal.
- Nash equilibria are generally not interchangeable.
