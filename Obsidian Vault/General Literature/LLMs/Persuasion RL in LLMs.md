- The persuader LLM aims to generate a message $m$ such that $m \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot \mid q, O, a_0, r_0, t)$.  Here, $(q,O)$ is the question and answer set, $a_o, r_o$ is the answer and rationale given by the persuadee, and $t$ is the target answer that the persuader is trying to switch belief to. The persuadee then generates another answer $(a1, r1) ∼ πP (· | q, O, a0, r0, m)$, and success is measured as the indicator $\operatorname{Succ}(q,t,m) = \mathbf{1}[a_1 = t]$.
- The persuadee is a frozen policy, and $\pi_{\theta}$ is optimized using GRPO. The final reward is generated as a sum of the persuader reward, format reward, and length reward $r_{\text{persuasion}}(m) = \mathbf{1}[a_1 = t], \quad r_{\text{fmt}}(m) = \mathbf{1}[m \in \mathcal{F}], \quad r_{\text{len}}(m) = \min\left(\frac{|m|}{L^\star}, 1\right), \quad R(m) = r_{\text{persuasion}}(m) + r_{\text{fmt}}(m) + r_{\text{len}}(m)$.
- Very interesting result, training the persuader cold-start against frontier models doesn't yield much result, but warm-starting it on open-source models and then training for a single epoch against frontier models marks a significant improvement.
